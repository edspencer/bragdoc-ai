The screenshots folder: choose a folder in Settings → Sources and BragDoc watches it for new screenshots, reads each one on your computer (no AI, nothing sent anywhere), and sends the ones that are praise for you to Found, with the picture, the words on a sticky and the rules they passed. The rest are only counted ("40 looked at · 3 sent to Found"). Off until you turn it on; only new files are read unless you ask it to look at the last 7 days; on a Mac it explains the Desktop permission prompt and how to give screenshots a folder of their own. Pictures you paste or drop on the board are read in the background too, so their words are searchable, and when they're praise for you the composer offers the quote as a sticky.
