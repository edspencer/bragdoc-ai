"Tidy this up" on the board, for when AI and Tidy are both switched on. Paste something messy (a chat message, a PR description, a rambling note) or a screenshot, press Tidy this up, and you get a suggested title, summary, date, impact and projects beside what you wrote. Use any field, or all of them; nothing is saved until you pin it, and what you wrote is kept in the notes. It uses only what's in your text: any number, date or name in the suggestion that isn't in your text is underlined, a date your text doesn't support is left out, and something that isn't an achievement (a calendar invite, say) is called that. It works in the edit drawer too. With AI or Tidy off, there's no button at all.
