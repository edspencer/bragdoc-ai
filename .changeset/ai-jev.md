The Found judge can use Jev, TypeSafe's yes-or-no decision model, through your OpenRouter key: add it in Settings → AI, read what it would send and allow it (its own consent, separate from OpenRouter's), then "Use for the judge". Jev answers with a probability only ("Looks like a win · 94%") and never says why, and BragDoc never makes a reason up for it. It can't write, so Tidy and Draft never use it.
