Folio is for the moment you need it all in one place: a self-review, a promotion case, a CV update, a one-to-one. Open
it from **Folio** in the header.

1. **Pick a window:** this quarter, last quarter, the last six months, this year, last year, or any two dates. "This
   quarter" and "this year" run to today. With nothing picked, Folio shows the last six months.
2. **Filter:** by project (including "No project"), by company, or by minimum impact. "At least 5" leaves out
   achievements you haven't rated; at the far left, everything counts, rated or not. Only projects and companies with
   something in the window are offered (projects with a count).
3. **Choose the clippings:** every matching achievement is listed, best first (highest impact, then newest). Untick
   the ones to leave out, or use **Tick all** and **Untick all**. Unticked ones stay in the list, faded, so you can
   change your mind. A long list shows the best ones first, with **Show all** for the rest.
4. **Read the preview:** the right-hand side shows the document as a printout, or as the raw Markdown. Group it by
   **project** (the default), **month** or **week**; grouped by month or week, each line names its projects.
5. **Take it away:** **Export .md** downloads `bragdoc-<from>-<to>.md`; **Copy** puts the same Markdown on the
   clipboard; **Print** prints just the printout, black on white, with each link's address written out (or save it as
   a PDF from your browser's print dialog). If your browser won't let BragDoc use the clipboard, it says so and points
   you at **Export .md**.

Everything you pick, the printout or Markdown view included, lives in the page's address, so reloading, going back, or bookmarking it brings the same folio
back.

## What's in the document

```md
# Alex Marlowe: achievements

_1 July 2026 – 30 September 2026 · 12 achievements_

## Pre-flight checklist (Altimetry)

- **Offline sync survived its first real weekend** · 13 Sep 2026 · impact 8/10
  Crews ran 140 checklists on flaky Wi-Fi.
  - PR: [altimetry-example/preflight-web#341: CRDT merge](https://github.com/altimetry-example/preflight-web/pull/341)
  - Screenshot: sync-dashboard.png
  > Not one conflict all weekend.
  >
  > — Imani Okafor, #preflight
```

- The heading uses the name in your profile; without one, it's just "Achievements". When you've filtered, a line under
  the dates says how.
- Each achievement has its title, its date (or date range), its impact if you've rated it, and its summary (or, if it
  has none, the first paragraph of its notes).
- **Pull requests and links stay links.** Quotes become blockquotes.
- **Files are named, never located.** Screenshots, PDFs and other files appear by their caption or file name only:
  no folder, and no link to BragDoc, which nobody you send the document to could open.
- **Nothing that only works on your computer or your network** goes in: a link to `localhost`, a private address
  (`192.168.…`, `10.…`) or a local name (`.local`, `.lan`, `.internal`, `.home.arpa`) is named and marked as left out, and links that aren't web
  pages (`mailto:`, `file:`) are dropped. The same goes for text you wrote: a local link in a summary or a quote
  becomes "(a local link)", and a file's location (`/Users/…`, `~/…`, `C:\…`) becomes just its name. Internal ids
  never appear.

Folio writes nothing to your [data folder](/reference/data-folder/): the document is made in your browser.

## The plain document, and an optional draft

Copy, Print and Export .md give exactly what you picked, in order, nothing added or rewritten: the facts to write your
review from. That never changes, and it's always the default.

If you turn on AI and its **Draft with AI** feature (Settings → AI; both are off until you do: see
[AI is opt-in](/guarantees/ai-is-opt-in/)), Folio also offers **Draft with AI** beside Copy and Export, with an
optional "What's this for?" note (a self-review, a promotion case, a 1:1). It writes a first draft from the same picks,
highest impact first, grouped by theme, in a Draft panel beside the printout and the markdown:

- **Every bullet cites what it came from** as `[3]`; click one to find that achievement in your list.
- **It's checked line by line.** Anything a line states that isn't in what it cites (a number, a date, a name) is
  flagged, as are bullets that cite nothing and stock phrases like "went above and beyond". Click a flag to select its
  line. It's a suggestion: you edit it, then **Copy draft** or **Export draft .md** (`bragdoc-draft-<from>-<to>.md`).
- **Only what the folio picks is sent**, cut down the same way as the document: no ids, no file locations, no links to
  your computer. If that's more than the model can read at once, the lowest-impact achievements are left out, and the
  panel says how many.
- **Nothing is saved.** The draft lives in the page; leaving Folio with one you haven't copied or exported asks first.
  Cancel stops it at once; a model on a computer without a GPU can take a few minutes.

With AI or Draft off, Folio looks and works exactly as it did before: no button, no hint.
