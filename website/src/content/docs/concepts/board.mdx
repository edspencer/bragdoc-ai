The board is where your achievements live: one card each, grouped by week (weeks start on Monday), newest first, in
one, two or three columns depending on the width of your window. Older weeks load as you scroll.

## Adding something

At the top is a box that says **Drop it here.** You can:

- **type** a line or a few: the first line is the title, the rest is the body;
- **paste** (⌘V or Ctrl+V) a screenshot or any image, or a link (one per paste; a link you type into the text stays
  part of the text);
- **drag and drop** images, PDFs or any other file, anywhere on the page;
- **pick files** with **Add a PDF or file**; **Paste a screenshot** and **Paste a PR link** take what's on your
  clipboard.

Each file can be up to 25 MB. A folder can't be added (zip it first).

A pasted GitHub pull request URL becomes a pull request slab. If you've [connected GitHub](/concepts/found-and-sources/#github),
BragDoc asks GitHub for its title; otherwise it shows as `owner/repo#123`. If that pull request is already on your
board, the box says so. Any other URL becomes a link, and BragDoc never fetches it.

Pictures are read on your machine (OCR) in the background after you add them, so the words in a screenshot become
searchable. If the words look like praise for you, the box offers **+ Add the quote as a sticky**. It decides with the
same plain rules as the [screenshots folder](/concepts/found-and-sources/#a-screenshots-folder), so it needs your name
in [your profile](/getting-started/quickstart/#2-say-who-you-are-or-skip-it). OCR reads PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP
pictures.

Before you pin it, pick its [projects](/concepts/projects/) and repos, how big it was (**How big?**: an impact from 1
to 10, optional) and when it happened (**When?**: today unless you change it). Then press **Pin it**, or ⌘+Enter
(Ctrl+Enter).

If you've turned on AI and its optional [**Tidy this up**](/ai/tidy/) feature, the box also has a **Tidy this up**
button: a model suggests a title, summary, date, impact and projects from what you typed or the picture's text, and
you use them field by field. Nothing is saved until you pin it. With Tidy off (the default), there's no button.

## The cards

A card shows its date, title, summary and evidence: screenshots as photo prints (click for a larger view), PDFs and
files as chips, pull requests as dark slabs, quotes as sticky notes. The tape across the top is in the colour of its
first project. A badge says how it got there when you didn't add it yourself: **Written by Claude Code** (or whichever
agent wrote it), **From GitHub**, **Spotted on your desktop** (the screenshots folder), or **Imported**.

Click a card to open it in a drawer, where you can change its title, summary, notes, dates (including an end date,
**Until**, for something that took a while), impact, projects and repos, and add or remove evidence: files, links and
quotes. From there:

- **Archive** hides it from the board but keeps it. The **Archived** filter shows archived cards, and **Unarchive**
  brings one back.
- **Remove** moves its file into `backups/removed/` in your data folder rather than erasing it. The message that
  appears has an **Undo**.

If the file behind a card can't be read (a broken hand edit), or was written by a newer version of BragDoc, the card
says so and can't be edited until that's sorted out; see [Your data is files](/concepts/your-data-is-files/#safe-by-design).

## Search and live updates

The search box searches titles, summaries, notes, evidence titles and links, quotes (and who said them), file names,
and the text read from pictures. The search is kept in the page's address (`?q=`), so you can bookmark it.

The board updates live when its files change on disk, whether the change came from your editor, a `git pull`, or an
agent.

When sources have spotted things, a strip at the top says how many are waiting in [Found](/concepts/found-and-sources/).
If a file in your data folder has a problem, a banner says so and links to Settings → Data.
