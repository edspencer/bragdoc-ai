**AI is off by default, and BragDoc works fully without it.** Pasting, Found, the board, Projects and Folio all work
with AI off. While it's off, no model is asked anything, on this computer or anywhere else, and BragDoc doesn't even
load the AI library it talks to models with. Upgrading to a version with AI changes nothing until you act.

There are two levels of switch, and each one starts off:

1. **The master switch**, in **Settings → AI**. Turning it on lets you set up where models run. It sends nothing by
   itself, and it switches no feature on.
2. **A switch per feature.** Each of the three features below has its own, on its card in Settings → AI.

## The three features

| Feature | Where | What it does | When it sends |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**The judge**](/ai/the-judge/) | Found | Reads a candidate the rules already let through and says whether it looks like a win for you, how sure it is, and why. | When you press **Judge now**, or on a schedule you choose. |
| [**Tidy this up**](/ai/tidy/) | The board | Turns something you pasted, or a picture's text, into a suggested title, summary, date, impact and projects. | Only when you press **Tidy this up**. |
| [**Draft with AI**](/ai/draft/) | Folio | Writes a review draft from the achievements a folio picks, citing each one. | Only when you press **Draft with AI**. |

Turning a feature on is what makes its button appear: with Tidy off there's no Tidy button on the board, with Draft
off there's no Draft in Folio, and with the judge off Found has no Judge now (and, until the judge has ever run, no
verdicts). When a feature is on but can't run yet (no model chosen, a runner that isn't answering, a cloud
connection you haven't allowed), Tidy's and Draft's buttons show disabled, with the reason and a link to Settings → AI;
Found leaves out Judge now, and the judge's card in Settings → AI says why.

**AI suggests; you decide.** Nothing a model writes reaches your board, an achievement or a file of yours without a
click from you:

- the judge only ever fills in its opinion on a Found candidate. It never keeps anything and never dismisses anything;
- Tidy's suggestion sits beside your text, and you use it field by field. Nothing is saved until you pin it;
- a draft lives in the Folio page, for you to edit, copy or export. Nothing is written to your data folder.

**Nothing invented, checked in code.** Each prompt tells the model to use only what it was given, and BragDoc then
checks the answer: a number, an amount or a capitalised name in a suggestion that isn't in what you gave it is
flagged, a date your text doesn't support is left out, and a judge's quote that isn't word for word in the screenshot
is dropped.

## Where the models run

A model runs on a **connection**: somewhere models run. You add connections in Settings → AI:

- **On this computer, or your network.** A model runner such as [Ollama, LM Studio or llama.cpp](/ai/local-models/).
  It needs no consent and no key: nothing leaves your network.
- **In the cloud**, with your own key: [Anthropic](/ai/cloud/anthropic/), [OpenAI](/ai/cloud/openai/),
  [OpenRouter](/ai/cloud/openrouter/), [Jev](/ai/cloud/jev/) through OpenRouter, or
  [any other server](/ai/cloud/custom/) that speaks OpenAI's API.

**Whether a connection is local or cloud is decided by its address, never by what kind it is.** It counts as local
only when its host is this computer (`localhost`, `127.0.0.1`, `::1`), a private network address (`10.x`, `172.16–31.x`,
`192.168.x`, link-local, IPv6 unique-local), a `.local` name or `host.docker.internal`. Every other host is the
internet, including an "OpenAI-compatible" server you typed in yourself, and **a connection on the internet sends
nothing at all, not even a test, until you've read exactly what it would send and pressed Allow**. Each provider's
page has that statement word for word.

[Choosing a model](/ai/choosing-a-model/) has the recommended models, the RAM each wants, and what they scored.

## What each feature sends

What a model is sent is built by the same code that shows it to you: each feature's card in Settings → AI has a
"what's sent" button (**Show me what's sent** for the judge, **See exactly what's sent** for Tidy and Draft), and so
does each judged card in Found (**What's sent**). It shows the exact prompt and sends nothing: for the judge, the one
it would send for a real candidate of yours; for Tidy and Draft, the instructions with a placeholder where your text
or your folio's achievements go. In short:

- **The judge:** the screenshot's text (read on this computer), with your own messages marked; for a merged pull
  request, if you tick that, its repository, title, merge date and the first 2,000 characters of its description; your
  name and role, the other names you go by and the names to ignore; your active and paused projects' names; and your
  [what counts as a win](/ai/the-judge/#what-counts-as-a-win-for-me) instructions.
- **Tidy:** the text you pasted, or the text read from a picture you added (or, from an achievement's edit drawer, its
  title, summary, notes and quotes); today's date; your name and role; your
  projects' names; and your optional "how I write" notes.
- **Draft:** the achievements in the folio (titles, summaries, dates, impact, project and company names, and their
  evidence: a quote's words and who said it, a pull request's number and title, a link's site and title, a file's
  name); the folio's dates; your name and role; your note on what the draft is for; and your "how I write" notes.

Never sent: file paths, BragDoc's ids, or BragDoc's own addresses. The picture itself never goes to a model either:
the judge and Tidy read the text BragDoc read from it, on your machine.

## Seeing what it did

Every call to a model is a line in **Settings → Network activity**, stamped *this computer*, *your network* or
*internet*, with the feature, the model and how many bytes were sent (never the content). The receipt there also
totals what went **to AI providers on the internet**, beside the unchanged **To BragDoc's makers: 0 B**.

**AI activity**, at the bottom of Settings → AI, is a receipt of runs: when, what started it, which model, where, how
many items and what came of them, and how long it took. It's kept in `logs/ai-runs.jsonl` in your data folder, and
it never holds content either.

## Turning it off

Switching the master switch off stops anything running at once, and hides every AI button in the app again. A
verdict the judge already wrote stays on its Found card (it's data, like anything else you have), marked "judged
earlier". Your connections, keys and instructions stay where they are, unused, until you turn AI on again.

To stop one cloud provider without turning AI off, press **Revoke** on its connection: nothing more is sent to it from
that moment.

The promise behind all this, rule by rule, is on [AI is opt-in](/guarantees/ai-is-opt-in/).
