What you paste in a hurry is rarely what you'd want in a review: a run-on line, a forwarded message, a screenshot of
someone thanking you. **Tidy this up** asks a model to turn it into a suggested **title**, **summary**, **date**,
**impact** (with a one-line reason) and **projects**, which you then use field by field, or not at all.

## Turning it on

Switch on **Tidy this up, on the board** in Settings → AI (with AI on and a model set up). Then a **Tidy this up**
button appears:

- **in the composer** on the board, once it has text, or a picture whose text has been read;
- **in the edit drawer** of an achievement you already have. It tidies the achievement as last saved (its title,
  summary, notes, and the words of its quotes and read pictures), and says so if you have unsaved edits.

With Tidy off, there's no button, and the composer is exactly as it was.

## A suggestion, beside your text

The suggestion opens beside what you wrote, with **Use** on each field, **Use all**, and **Keep mine**. **Cancel**
works while it's thinking.

- **Nothing is saved** until you press **Pin it** (or **Save** in the edit drawer), as always.
- **What you wrote survives Use all.** In the composer, using a suggested title moves what you'd typed down into the
  notes under it, so your own words are kept with the achievement. In the drawer, a suggested body goes above your
  notes.
- An impact you accept is yours, like any impact you set.
- If it doesn't think the text is an achievement at all (a calendar invite, say), it says so, and offers nothing to
  use. If it couldn't tell something ("who 'we' is"), it says that too.

## Nothing invented

The model is told to use only the facts in your text, keep your voice and first person, and leave a field empty rather
than guess. BragDoc then checks what came back:

- **Any number, amount, percentage or capitalised name that isn't in your text is underlined**: "not in your text.
  Check it, or leave it out." Your name, the names you go by, your role and your project names count as yours.
- **A date is kept only if your text states it**, or a relative date in it ("yesterday", "last week", "March 4")
  resolves to it. Any other date is left out, and it says so.
- Titles, summaries and bodies are cut to length at a word, and project names that aren't yours are dropped.

## What it sends

The text you pasted, or the text read from a picture you added (read on your machine: the picture itself is never
sent); today's date; your name and role; your projects' names; BragDoc's 1–10 impact scale; and your **how I write**
notes, if you wrote some. Tidy takes up to 10,000 characters, so a tidy fits a small local model: given more, it
says so and asks you to tidy the part that matters, or trim it first.

**How I write** is an optional Markdown file, `ai/style.md` in your data folder, with an editor on Tidy's card (Draft
uses it too). Write in it how you like your achievements phrased.

**See exactly what's sent** on Tidy's card shows the exact instructions, with a placeholder where your text goes, and
sends nothing.
Each tidy is a line in AI activity and in Network activity, without its content.
