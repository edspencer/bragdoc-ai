[Folio](/concepts/folio/) gathers the achievements in a date window, filtered as you like, and exports exactly what you
picked. **Draft with AI** is an optional extra beside it: a model writes a first draft of the achievements section of a
self-review, a promotion case or a 1:1, from those same achievements.

**The plain export is unchanged, and stays the default.** Copy, Print and Export .md still give exactly what you picked,
with nothing added or rewritten.

## Turning it on

Switch on **Draft with AI, in Folio** in Settings → AI (with AI on and a model set up). Folio then shows **Draft with
AI**, with an optional **What's this for?** note (self-review, promotion case, 1:1…), beside Copy, Print and Export.
With Draft off, Folio is exactly as it was.

## The draft

The draft streams into a **Draft** view beside Printout and Markdown, with how long it's taken and **Cancel**. It's a
short opening paragraph, then themed sections of bullets, highest impact first, and **every bullet cites the
achievements it came from**: `[3]` links back to that clipping in the list, and lights it up.

Then it's yours to edit, with **Copy draft**, **Export draft .md** (`bragdoc-draft-<from>-<to>.md`), **Draft again**
and **Discard**.

- **Nothing is saved.** Like the folio itself, a draft lives in the page, and nothing is written to your data folder.
  Leaving Folio (or reloading) with a draft you haven't copied or exported asks first.
- **A draft you have is yours.** If AI is switched off mid-draft, it stops, and what arrived stays editable, copyable
  and exportable until you leave.

## Checked, line by line

The model is told to use only what's in the clippings. BragDoc then checks every line against **the clippings that
line cites**, not just "somewhere in the folio", and lists what to look at:

- a number, date or capitalised name that isn't in the clippings it cites ("Not in the clippings it cites");
- a bullet with no citation ("No citation"), or a citation to something that wasn't sent;
- a short list of filler phrases, unless your own achievements use them.

Click one to select its line. The check runs again as you edit, against the draft's own numbering, and makes no model
call.

## What it sends

The folio exactly as the page shows it: BragDoc rebuilds the clippings on its side from the folio's window and filters,
so nothing else can slip in. That's each achievement's title, summary, dates, impact, project and company names, and
its evidence by name, numbered best first. Plus your name and role, your **What's this for?** note, and your
**how I write** notes (`ai/style.md`, shared with [Tidy](/ai/tidy/)). Only the projects and companies of the
achievements sent are named, never the rest of your list. Paths, ids and BragDoc's addresses are never in a clipping.

**It fits the model.** With a local model's default context of 8,192 tokens, about 5,000 go to the clippings (about
25,000 with a cloud model). A bigger folio leaves out its lowest-impact achievements, tells the model it's not
everything, and tells you: "12 lower-impact items weren't sent (the 40 highest-impact ones were, to fit the model's
context)", with how to include them: narrow the window or the projects, untick some, or use a model with a larger
context.

On a CPU with no GPU, a draft takes a few minutes (it's given up to 15; a cloud model, 3).

**See exactly what's sent** on Draft's card shows the exact prompt for a folio of your data, and sends nothing. Each
draft is one line in AI activity, and its calls are in Network activity, without their content.
